            💸 UPI Safety
            I never share my UPI PIN with anyone
            UPI PIN is like ATM PIN — no bank or app asks for it
            I check the payee name before confirming any UPI payment
            Scammers create similar-looking names to deceive
            I know that entering PIN is needed to SEND, not RECEIVE money
            You never need to enter PIN to receive money via UPI
            I use UPI apps only from official app stores
            Sideloaded APKs may contain keyloggers
            📱 SIM Security
            I have enabled SIM lock / PIN on my phone
            Prevents SIM swap attacks
            I never share my Aadhaar-linked phone OTP with anyone
            Scammers use OTP for unauthorized SIM port
            I know my carrier's SIM swap verification process
            Carriers require biometric for SIM swap in India
            📲 App Security
            I never install apps from links sent over WhatsApp/SMS
            Malicious APKs can steal banking credentials
            I have never installed AnyDesk or QuickSupport for a stranger
            Screen sharing apps let fraudsters control your phone
            I review app permissions before granting access
            Flashlight app doesn't need SMS permission
            I keep all apps and OS updated
            Security patches fix vulnerabilities exploited by malware
            I use a trusted antivirus on my Android device
            Detects malware before it steals banking data
            🔑 Account Security
            I use strong, unique passwords for all banking accounts
            Never reuse passwords across banking apps
            I have enabled 2FA on email and banking apps
            Two-factor authentication prevents account takeover
            I check my bank statements regularly for unauthorized transactions
            Early detection is key to fraud recovery
            I never save card details on unknown websites
            Only save on trusted platforms with PCI-DSS compliance
            I verify sender name before clicking any bank email link
